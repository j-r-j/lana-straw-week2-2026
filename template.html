Was That a Real Trade?!

The Kickers n Jigba landed Bijan. The Shiny Hineys left Atlanta. Both clubs woke up 2-0. The poll did not.

The blockbuster · Thursday, Sept. 17

Thursday at 8:20 p.m. Boom Baum saw the Bijan deal and said it out loud. “Holy hell. Was that a real trade?!” Eleven seconds later he answered himself. “Holy shit.”

The Shiny Hineys had paid $69 for Bijan Robinson at the auction, then watched him score 27.3 in a Week 1 win, 93.56 to 90.50 over Backdoor Heat. Thursday night they shipped him. The Kickers n Jigba took Bijan, Chris Olave and Zach Charbonnet. The Hineys walked back with Davante Adams, Javonte Williams, Braelon Allen and Garrett Wilson. Somebody forgot a leg. The Hineys did not. “the bot forgot the $5 FAAB Rob gave me. Really made the trade”

That afternoon the pod had already put out S05 EP03, Week 1 Dont Overreact (I’m Going to Overreact). The guest was the Guy Next Door, who had already failed the assignment on his own Week 1. On the show he said he had told himself, out loud, more than once, “I’m not going to overreact.” The Bijan deal arrived after the episode. It was not his trade. It followed the title anyway.

He’s Gumby. The most durable RB I’ve ever seen. I’m also tired of watching Falcon games. Shiny Hineys · Thursday, Sept. 17, 8:55 p.m.

At 9:02 the Hineys were not done. “Fading the reigning MVP is certainly a choice.” Fart Toot Squeek had already said his piece, at 8:52.

it feels like your relationship with Bijan is a love/hate thing. That’s the only way I can make sense of you shipping him away year after year Fart Toot Squeek · to the Shiny Hineys · Thursday, Sept. 17, 8:52 p.m.

Boom wanted the same treatment. At 8:37 p.m. he told the Jigba, “your my new favorite to win it all.” Friday afternoon he asked the Hineys, “can you give me all your good players like you did Rob?”

Sunday settled the week. The who-won poll never closed. Adams scored 36.5 from the Hineys’ flex. Bijan scored 9.6 in his Jigba debut, started. Charbonnet scored 0.0 on the injured list. Olave scored 18.6. Josh Allen scored 43.82 and Jaxon Smith-Njigba scored 40.0 on the same Jigba roster. The Hineys beat Boom Baum 112.32 to 89.22. The Jigba beat the Scott Ramsays 144.62 to 76.68. Both clubs are 2-0. The Jigba leads the Premier at 324.5 points for. Sunday night the Hineys named the Boom matchup. “Your weekly pillow fight sponsored by Boom Baum and Shiny Hineys.”

The Year That Started Early

Fart Toot Squeek installed 2027’s kicker scoring in 2026, said the correction would flip a Champeens game, and then went to 0-2.

The apology · Monday night, Sept. 15

Monday at 9:50 p.m., Fart Toot Squeek confessed. Eight people let it stand. On Thursday’s pod he called it “the big screw up of updating the kicker scoring.”

Commissioner’s Statement

“@everyone I regret to inform y’all that I’ve made a grave mistake. For those who aren’t aware at our Rules Round Table at the draft weekend we voted to change the kicker scoring starting in 2027(decreasing made 40yd+ and 50yd+ FGs by 1pt). I totally forgot about the delayed onset of the rule change and implemented it this season. Y’all will see it change some scores slightly and much to my chagrin it will flip the outcome of Jerry GM and Dave Ten Haves game. I’m going to do some push ups, run 10 40s, and keep my head on a swivel so this never happens again. My deepest apologies Lana Straw”
